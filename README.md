# Irish Graduate Outcomes Analysis 🎓💼📈

This project presents a statistical analysis of employment and salary outcomes for higher education graduates in Ireland. Using data from the Central Statistics Office (CSO), the project explores how variables like academic performance, institution tier, gender, and field of study impact job placement and income levels.

## 📌 Key Objectives

- Understand the correlation between academic performance and employment
- Assess gender-based employment and salary differences
- Compare graduate outcomes by institutional tiers
- Identify industry and sector employment patterns
- Evaluate salary trends across fields and NFQ levels

## 🔍 Methods Used

- Descriptive statistics
- Two-proportion and two-mean Z-tests
- Confidence intervals
- ANOVA and non-pooled T-tests
- Correlation T-tests
- Visual data summaries

## 🧪 Tools & Technologies

- Excel (for data handling and statistical testing)
- Python (optional future extension)
- CSO & HEA data
- Academic statistical principles

## 📊 Insights Gained

- Employment rates are not significantly influenced by academic grades
- Institutional tier affects employment but not salary
- NFQ level positively correlates with earnings
- No statistically significant gender pay gap was found
- Arts and Services fields show lower salary growth compared to ICT and Business
